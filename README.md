**# IPL 2022 Data Analysis**



**## Project Overview**

This project focuses on analysing ball-by-ball data from the Indian Premier League (IPL) 2022 season.



The main aim is to explore team and player performances, identify important statistics and understand patterns in the tournament using Python and data visualization libraries.



**## Tools and Technologies Used**

\- Python

\- Pandas – Data cleaning and analysis

\- NumPy – Numerical operations

\- Matplotlib – Data visualization

\- Seaborn – Statistical data visualization



**## Dataset**

The project uses an IPL 2022 ball-by-ball dataset containing information such as:

\- Batting team

\- Batsman and bowler

\- Runs scored

\- Extras

\- Wicket deliveries

\- Dismissal types

\- Match and innings details



**## Analysis Performed**

The following questions were explored:



1\. Which team scored the highest total runs?

2\. Who were the top 10 run-scoring batsmen?

3\. Which bowlers recorded the most wicket deliveries?

4\. Which dismissal type occurred most frequently?

5\. How many runs did each team score in each innings?

6\. What were the strike rates of qualified batsmen (minimum 50 balls faced)?

7\. Which bowlers had the lowest economy rates (minimum 60 legal balls)?

8\. Who scored the highest individual runs in a single innings?

9\. What was the average score of each team per innings?



**## Key Findings**

\- Rajasthan Royals scored the highest total runs.

\- Jos Buttler was the leading run-scoring batsman.

\- Yuzvendra Chahal recorded the highest number of wicket deliveries.

\- Caught was the most common dismissal type, with 625 occurrences.

\- Sunrisers Hyderabad had the highest average score in the first innings (181.67).

\- Gujarat Titans had the highest average score in the second innings (169.67).



**## Visualizations**

The project includes the following visualizations:



1\. Total runs scored by each team.

2\. Top 10 run-scoring batsmen.

3\. Top 10 bowlers by wicket deliveries.

4\. Team-wise average runs in the first and second innings.



The charts are available in the `visualizations` folder.
